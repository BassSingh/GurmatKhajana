Why Study Gurbani Grammar?

There is an urgent need to raise awareness of the importance of studying Gurbani and its grammar (Viakaran). Understanding grammar helps us understand the true meaning and pronunciation of Gurbani.

ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ
One of the first questions students ask is: Why is the Sihari written but not pronounced? Gurbani Grammar provides the rules that explain these patterns and helps us understand both pronunciation and meaning.
