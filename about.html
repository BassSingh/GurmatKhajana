About GurmatKhajana

A living digital archive dedicated to preserving Gurmat writings, Jeevani, Naam Abhiyaas, Gursikhi history and spiritual reflections for generations to come.

Our Vision

Preserving Gurmat Legacy

GurmatKhajana has been created with the aim of preserving priceless Gurmat writings, historical articles, Gursikh Jeevan experiences and spiritual treasures that may otherwise become difficult to access over time.

This archive is intended to provide a clean, organised and accessible platform for seekers wishing to explore Naam Abhiyaas, Gurmat philosophy, Jeevani and the writings of inspiring Gursikhs.
