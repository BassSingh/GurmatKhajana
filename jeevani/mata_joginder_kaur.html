Mata Joginder Kaur Ji

A Devoted Servant of Waheguru

Back to Jeevani

A Gursikh whose love for Waheguru, devotion to Gurbani, and connection with the sangat of the Akhand Kirtani Jatha left a quiet and lasting mark on all those whose lives she touched.

Mata Joginder Kaur Ji lived a jeevan rooted in Naam, Rehat, and sincere love for Guru Sahib. Those who knew her remember not outward display, but the quiet steadiness of a soul deeply attached to Waheguru — a presence that brought warmth, calm, and spiritual sincerity wherever she went.

Her jeevan was one of devotion lived in the everyday — within family, within sangat, and within the heart. Through the decades, her connection to the Akhand Kirtani Jatha and the Gursikhs she met along the way shaped and deepened her path in ways she would always remember with gratitude.

Amrit and the Search for Sangat

In 1968, after receiving Amrit alongside her husband, Swaran Singh Malhi, Mata Joginder Kaur Ji felt a deep desire to meet fellow Gursikhs. This search led them to the Akhand Kirtani Jatha, where they were deeply moved by the sangat's devotion to Gurbani and their unwavering commitment to the three pillars of Sikhi — earning an honest living, serving others selflessly, and cultivating spiritual awareness through Naam Simran.

It was during this time that they first heard of a revered Gursikh named Bau Mal Singh Ji from Tarn Taran — a former military man, a lifelong companion of Bhai Sahib Randhir Singh Ji of Narangwal, and after Bhai Sahib's passing, his successor as head of the AKJ.

A Spiritual Encounter

In 1968, Mata Joginder Kaur Ji and Papaji travelled to visit Bau Mal Singh Ji at his home in Tarn Taran. Upon their arrival, they were astonished when Bau Ji approached her with profound bairaag, his eyes filled with tears — despite this being their very first meeting, and she being a complete stranger to him.

"Where have you been? I have been waiting for you for many years."

Bau Mal Singh Ji, upon first meeting Mata Joginder Kaur Ji

With deep emotion, Bau Ji looked at her and asked where she had been, saying he had waited many years. When Mata Joginder Kaur Ji, bewildered, asked what he meant, Bau Ji replied with quiet certainty — in their previous life, she had been his mother.

This deeply spiritual encounter marked the beginning of a profound connection between Mata Joginder Kaur Ji, Papaji, and Bau Mal Singh Ji's family. Their bond grew so strong that Bau Ji, in a gesture of great love and respect, gifted them a sarab loh mala and a bata — items that had once been given to him personally by Bhai Randhir Singh Ji himself. This spiritual connection remained a cherished part of her life, and the relationship between the two families deepened in ways that words can scarcely express.

Mata Joginder Kaur Ji's jeevan stands as a reminder that Waheguru's hand is present in every sincere soul's path — drawing together those bound across lifetimes through love, devotion, and the blessed company of Gursikhs.
